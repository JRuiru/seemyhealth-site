# Site Improvement Suggestions

## High Impact — Conversion

- [ ] **Sticky "Add to Cart" bar on product pages** — as users scroll past the hero, a slim bar with price + CTA stays visible (already exists on configure pages via `StickyAddToCart`)
- [ ] **Product comparison table** — side-by-side specs for Scale vs Scale Pro, or all 6 devices, to help undecided buyers
- [ ] **Social proof counters** — "X people viewing this" or "Y sold this week" near the buy button

## Visual Polish

- [ ] **Prefers-reduced-motion support** — disable GSAP/ScrollTrigger animations in `animations.ts` when user prefers reduced motion
- [ ] **Loading skeletons** — for images in gallery/lifestyle sections to prevent layout shift
- [ ] **Video autoplay on product hero** — short looping clips instead of static images for above-the-fold on desktop

## Trust & Conversion

- [ ] **FAQ accordion per product page** — FAQs exist on support page but not inline on product pages where buying decisions happen
- [ ] **"What's in the box" section on product pages** — exists on configure page but not on the main product page
- [ ] **Shipping & returns info** — small note near the price ("Free shipping" / "30-day returns") to build purchase confidence

## SEO & Growth

- [ ] **Blog / content section** — educational posts about sleep tracking, body composition, hydration science to drive organic traffic
- [ ] **Email capture** — subtle popup or inline form for newsletter/launch updates (Klaviyo is already configured)
